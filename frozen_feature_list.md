# Frozen Feature List: SOL 7 — Mars Survival Trainer

**Target Audience:** Ages 7–12
**Platform:** Electron desktop (.exe) + browser build served over HTTP (WebGL, Three.js)

---

## Built (Protect at All Costs)
1. **Core resource system:** Oxygen, Water, Food, Base Power, Health and Radiation, with warnings below 25%. Every choice costs something: night outside means freezing and storms cut power.
2. **Full play loop:** Launch, then a player-flown landing, then Sol 1 salvage, then base building from a blueprint, then a seven-Sol story: storm damage, the long night, an isolation storm, a meteor shower, powering the capsule, and launching home. There is no rescue; the player unlocks data chips and programs the capsule to fly back.
3. **Real-science mechanics:** Martian gravity, ice melting with latent heat, MOXIE-style oxygen, greenhouse potatoes, dust storms, a meteor shower, RTG power, Phobos and Deimos at true sizes.
4. **Educational failure tone:** Death screens explain the cause (for example, freezing), and `R` restarts. Every run is a learn-and-retry.
5. **HMSA fact captions and subtitles** (the in-game agency is the fictional Huraira Meerab Space Agency; real data sources are credited in `credits.md`): Short in-game facts that also explain scientific terms as they come up (`M` cycles night-sky facts).
